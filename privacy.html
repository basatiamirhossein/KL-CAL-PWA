DataDev Pipe Weight Calculator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

  • The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.
  • All calculations run on your device; the app works without an internet connection.
  • Your settings (language, last pipe and the numbers you entered) are kept only in the device's local storage and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • The app contains no ads, trackers or analytics, and shares no data with third parties.
  • The DataDev link at the bottom of the app only opens datadevvv.ir in your browser.

Questions about this policy: contact the developer, Amirhossein Basati, via datadevvv.ir.
