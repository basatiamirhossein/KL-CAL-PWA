## Full description
DataDev Pipe Weight Calculator gives you the weight per metre, wall thickness, inside diameter and price of a pipe in seconds, straight from the standard tables. Built for contractors, pipe dealers, MEP engineers and buyers.

Supported pipes:
• Polyethylene PE80 / PE100 (ISO 4427, INSO 14427-2)
• Polypropylene PP-R (DIN 8077)
• PVC-U pressure pipe (ISO 1452) and ASTM D1785 Sch 40/80
• PVC drainage (EN 1401, EN 1329)
• PEX and PE-RT (ISO 22391, ISO 15875)
• CPVC, ABS and PVDF
• Copper (EN 1057, ASTM B88 Type K/L/M)
• Carbon and galvanised steel (EN 10255 L/M/H, ASME B36.10 Sch 40/80/160)
• Stainless steel grades 201, 304, 304L, 308, 309S, 310S, 316, 316L, 321 and 430 (ASME B36.19)
• "Other": any outside diameter and wall with 22 material densities (aluminium, brass, ductile iron, titanium…)

Features:
• Weight per metre, total weight, price per metre and total price
• Works with any currency — enter the price per kg in yours
• Full weight table for every standard
• Compare every pressure class of a size side by side
• 5 languages: English, فارسی, العربية, Türkçe, Deutsch
• Fully offline, no ads, no data collection
